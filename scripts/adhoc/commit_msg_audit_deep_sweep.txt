docs(audit): deep sweep — 4 critical new bugs surface

Section 13 of the audit doc captures a deeper investigation triggered
by "are there any other issues" - the answer is yes, several:

- 13.1 CRITICAL: SELL trades bypass order_intents tracking entirely.
  89 BUY intents in DB, 0 SELL intents, despite 19 SELL trades. The
  create_order_intent calls in the SELL paths never persist a row.
  Means SELLs are never broker-confirmed by verify_order_statuses.

- 13.2 CRITICAL: XLV phantom-trade chain. ML Momentum BUY 11 XLV
  on 2026-05-18 (intent stuck ACKED, never confirmed). Local SELL
  recorded on 2026-05-21 with +$24 P&L (no intent created at all).
  Broker SYNC on 2026-05-22 found XLV still in account at a different
  cost basis ($145.44 vs local $145.79), imported as BROKER_SYNC.
  The +$24 P&L is fictitious. Same pattern likely affects others.

- 13.3 CRITICAL: $36k cash-impact divergence. Trades table shows
  -$41,818 net cash impact over 14 days, actual cash dropped only
  -$5,543. ~$36k worth of BUYs are recorded locally but never
  actually executed at broker. The 31.6% win rate / +$204 P&L /
  live-readiness gate are all computed from possibly-fictitious
  trade rows.

- 13.4 HIGH: News Sentiment has the SAME naive time-exit anti-pattern
  ML Momentum had before commit 9f1471a. AMZN/ABBV opened 2026-05-27
  will force-exit on 2026-06-03 regardless of P&L unless fixed.

- 13.5: 4 unit tests failing - OPG order timing, two LTCG tax-aware
  exits, and a Python 3.9/3.10 compat issue.

- 13.6: Email script (scripts/generate_daily_email.py) carries the
  same latent shares > 0 short-position bug + reads the misleading
  equal capital_allocation DB column.

Section 14 has a combined priority-ordered fix list across both
audit passes.
